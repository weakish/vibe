# Chiang Mai group runs (weekly & monthly)

Monday
  16:30  CGH3 (Gentlemen's Hash) — men only,
         paper-trail hash + BYO circle,
         changing A-site
         Founded 26 Oct 2020. Songthaew 15:20
         Loi Kroh Soi 3, outside Monta Hotel
         (one listing 15:30). Bring
         beer/water/chair; no run fee,
         bus ~100 THB.
  17:00  CH3 (Hash House Harriers) — men only,
         paper-trail hash + paid circle,
         changing A-site
         Founded 1981; original Chiang Mai
         kennel, currently ~run #1666.
         Songthaew 15:50 in front of
         McDonald's, Tha Phae Gate. Run+bus
         150–300 THB.
  18:00  Doi Trail Research — easy social,
         CMU lake
         Meet Living a Dream cafe (Ang Kaew).
         Club site says 6PM; a 26 Oct 2026
         Reddit report said 17:30.

Tuesday
  06:45  Doi Trail Research — hill repeats /
         trail, Doi Suthep
         Meet Basecamp Coffee Club, 208 Suthep
         (Pilgrim / Bamboo trailheads).

Wednesday
  07:00  Doi Trail Research + TANN — easy
         ~5 km road, then coffee
         Meet TANN, Nimman Soi 5. Rain call
         ~06:00 on Instagram. Listed on
         Wingers for 18 Nov 2026; site weekly
         block currently shows only Mon/Tue.

Thursday
  07:00  JOG JOY JOIN — easy short/medium city
         Meet Chiang Mai Municipality /
         Ratchadamnoen.
         RunningCrews lists a weekly Thursday
         07:00; a May 2026 write-up said once
         a month. Confirm on Instagram.
  07:30  Go Light Run — CMU lake
         Meet opposite Living a Dream cafe
         (Ang Kaew). Local report (time/place);
         no public listing found.
  16:30  CH4 (Happy Hash) — mixed, paper-trail
         hash + circle, changing countryside
         A-site
         Songthaew 15:20 in front of
         McDonald's, Tha Phae Gate. Run+bus
         150–300 THB.
  18:00  4Seas Thursday Social Run — easy,
         conversational
         Meet at front entrance of 4Seas
         Nimman, Nimman Soi 15. Arrive 17:55.
         Free. Leader Yajas.

Saturday
  06:45  Faburunsclub — Crew Muang, easy
         ~5–6 km city
         Meet TANN, Nimman Soi 5. Free
         walk-in.
  08:30  Slowcial Running Club — easy 20–25 min
         / ~4–6 km, then coffee
         Meet opposite Living a Dream cafe,
         Ang Kaew / CMU. Free.
         Meetup clock for 21 Nov 2026 is
         08:30–10:30 ICT (winter postpone).
         Body text still says 7:30am;
         syndicators still show 07:30. Trust
         the Meetup time field / WhatsApp.
  16:30  CSH3 (Saturday Hash) — mixed,
         paper-trail hash + circle, changing
         countryside A-site
         Songthaew 15:20 in front of
         McDonald's, Tha Phae Gate. Run+bus
         150–300 THB.

Sunday
  17:00  Sunday Sunset Walk & Connect — walk,
         not a run
         CMU main gate bench, then campus /
         lake loop ~17:00–19:00. Host Fran /
         Beyond Small Talk. Meetup.
         Also listed as Mindful Sunset Walk.

Monthly
  2nd Tue     CDH3 Diamond Hash — women only.
              Homepage: second Tuesday;
              another kennel page says last
              Tuesday. Typical hash pickup
              McDonald's Tha Phae.
  Full moon   CFMH3 Full Moon Hash — mixed
              night run, 18:30. Founded 2025.
              No run fee; city A-site, no
              songthaew; BYO or buy drinks
              nearby; torch. Next listed:
              24 Nov 2026 Kad Kom Market;
              22 Dec 2026.
  Last Sun    CBH3 Bunny Hash — ladies'
              hash, visitors (men included)
              welcome. Founded 2001. Food,
              raffle, circle. Last Sunday of
              the month. Nov 2026 is Nash
              Hash weekend (22 Nov); next
              ordinary listing 27 Dec 2026
              run #289.
  Thu 07:00   JOG JOY JOIN — see Thursday.
              RunningCrews crew page says
              weekly; May 2026 write-up said
              one Thursday morning a month.
